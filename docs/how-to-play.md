# How to play

## Carving

Open the site and start carving. Your pumpkin autosaves in your browser as you
go, so a refresh won't lose it. On a phone, the tools sit below the pumpkin.

| Tool | Key | How to use it |
|------|-----|---------------|
| 🪚 Saw | `1` | Drag to cut all the way through. Close a loop and the piece inside falls out. |
| 🔩 Drill | `2` | Tap to punch a round hole. |
| ⭐ Cutters | `3` | Pick a shape (triangle, circle, diamond, eye, toothy grin, star, moon, heart, bat), then tap to punch it out. **Tilt** rotates the shape. |
| ✏️ Etcher | `4` | Drag to shave off the skin so the flesh glows through. Each new pass goes one step deeper and glows brighter. The fourth pass over the same spot breaks through and becomes a hole. |
| 🩹 Patch | `5` | Paint skin back over cuts and etching. |
| 🔄 Rotate | `6` | Drag to spin the pumpkin. With any other tool, you can also drag outside the pumpkin or right-drag to rotate. |

Other controls:

- **Size** slider, or `[` and `]`: brush or shape size.
- **↶ / ↷** buttons, or `Ctrl/Cmd+Z` and `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z`: undo and redo.
  Undoing a cut that made a piece fall out plays the fall backwards, so the
  piece flies back into its hole.
- **⌖** button: turn the pumpkin back to face you.
- Scroll or pinch to zoom.

Tips:

- Cut a ring around the stem to make a lid. It falls off, stem and all.
- Etching is good for shading, like eyebrows, wrinkles or scales, without
  weakening the pumpkin.
- Look behind and around the pumpkin. The candle shines out through the holes and
  throws your design onto the walls and table.

## Candle, pumpkin and scene

- **Candle:** six presets, or pick any color with the last swatch.
- **Pumpkin:** orange, deep orange, ghost white or green skin.
- **Scene:** Studio, Kitchen table, Witch's cottage, Graveyard, Pumpkin patch or
  Front porch.

All three are saved with the pumpkin.

## Saving and sharing

1. Name your pumpkin (optional) and press **Save & share**.
2. Copy the link. It opens a page where your pumpkin turns slowly in its scene,
   and it shows a preview image when pasted into chat apps.
3. Keep carving and press **Save changes** to update the same link.

There are no accounts. The browser you saved from remembers that the pumpkin is
yours, and **My pumpkins** lists everything you've saved on this device. If you
open someone else's pumpkin in the editor (the **Remix** button), saving creates
a new copy and leaves theirs alone.

Clearing your browser's site data forgets which pumpkins you can edit. The links
keep working, but you'd have to remix to make changes.

**New pumpkin** clears the board and starts over.

## Party game

### Hosting

1. Go to **Party game**, enter your name, and set up the room:
   - **Prompts:** pick a category. Every prompt is an adjective plus a subject,
     like "A lazy cat" or "A grumpy chef", so it's easy to carve.
     - 🎲 **Random** (default): a different category each round
     - 🐶 **Animals**, 🧑‍🍳 **Jobs**, 👻 **Monsters**, 🍕 **Food**, 🚀 **Things**
     - 😜 **Moods**: the mood is the whole point, as in "A jealous pumpkin"
     - ✍️ **Custom**: type your own prompts, one per line (up to 100). Inside
       jokes work well. Only the host can see the list, so it won't spoil
       anything. A game can't start with an empty list.
   - **Carving time:** 30 seconds to 10 minutes (default 2 minutes)
   - **Votes per player:** 1 to 10 (default 3)
   - **Room size:** 2 to 8 players (default 8)
   - **Voting time:** 15 seconds to 5 minutes (default 60 seconds)
2. Press **Create room** and share the 4-letter code, or press
   **Copy invite link**.
   Subjects and adjectives don't repeat within a game until a list runs out.
3. You can still change the settings in the lobby, including the category. Once at least two people
   are in, press **Start carving!**

The host is marked with a crown. If the host leaves, someone else takes over.

### Playing a round

1. **Carve.** Everyone gets the same prompt, such as "A sleepy owl",
   with a countdown. Press **I'm done** when you're happy. You can keep carving and
   **Resubmit** until time runs out. When the clock hits zero, whatever you have is
   submitted automatically. If everyone submits early, the round ends early.
2. **Vote.** All the pumpkins are shown without names. Tap one to look at it
   in 3D. Use **+** and **−** to hand out your votes. You can stack several on one
   pumpkin, but you can't vote for your own. Press **Lock in my votes** when you're
   done. Voting ends when everyone has locked in or the timer runs out.
3. **Results.** Pumpkins are ranked by votes, with names revealed and ties
   sharing a place. Press **Save my pumpkin & get a link** to keep yours as a
   normal shareable pumpkin.

The host can then start the **Next round** with a new prompt, or go **Back to
lobby** to change settings.

### Joining

Enter your name and the room code, or just open the invite link. Games that
have already started can't be joined. Wait for the host to return to the lobby.

If your connection drops or you refresh the page, you rejoin automatically in
the same seat.
